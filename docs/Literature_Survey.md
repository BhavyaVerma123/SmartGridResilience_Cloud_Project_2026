This document outlines the core operational and technical goals of the research initiative.Evaluate Fault-Tolerance and Recovery Capabilities: Systematically assess the resilience, failover speeds, and service recovery times of smart grid cloud infrastructures under simulated adversarial attacks and cascading infrastructure disruptions.  Deploy an Autonomous Multi-Agent System (MAS): Engineer and deploy a decentralized network of intelligent agents capable of continuous distributed monitoring, localized anomaly detection, and rapid, autonomous mitigation of critical cloud service disruptions without requiring human intervention.  Enforce Secure Operational Boundaries: Design and implement a zero-trust architecture enforcing strict authorization best practices, heavily leaning on the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) to restrict inter-agent communication and isolate compromised subsystems.
