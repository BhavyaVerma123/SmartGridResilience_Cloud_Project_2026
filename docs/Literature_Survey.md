# Project Objectives

The primary technical and operational objectives of this research initiative are defined as follows:

* **Evaluate Fault-Tolerance and Recovery Capabilities:** To systematically assess the resilience, failover speeds, and service recovery times of smart grid cloud infrastructures under simulated adversarial attacks and cascading infrastructure disruptions[cite: 1].
* **Deploy an Autonomous Multi-Agent System (MAS):** To engineer and deploy a decentralized network of intelligent agents capable of continuous distributed monitoring, localized anomaly detection, and rapid, autonomous mitigation of critical cloud service disruptions without requiring human intervention[cite: 1].
* **Enforce Secure Operational Boundaries:** To design and implement a zero-trust architecture enforcing strict authorization best practices, heavily leaning on the Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) to restrict inter-agent communication and isolate compromised subsystems[cite: 1].
