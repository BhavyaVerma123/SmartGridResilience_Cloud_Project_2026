# Research Gap

A thorough analysis of existing critical infrastructure protection frameworks reveals a major, persistent flaw: most cloud-integrated smart grid architectures rely almost entirely on **passive monitoring** and **reactive mitigation** paradigms[cite: 1]. Current literature heavily emphasizes historical anomaly logging and rule-based alerting systems that notify human operators after a breach or infrastructure failure has already propagated[cite: 1].

Furthermore, existing frameworks lack an integrated, autonomous testing-to-mitigation feedback loop[cite: 1]. They do not combine active, multi-agent autonomous resilience assessments with zero-trust least-privilege enforcement during runtime disruptions[cite: 1]. This leaves a severe operational gap where cascading cloud errors can bypass passive firewalls and compromise physical grid systems before human remediation can begin[cite: 1].
